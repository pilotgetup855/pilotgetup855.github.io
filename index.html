❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know programming to use this?

Absolutely not. bbshare-skills is built for everyone. If you can click a button and type, you can use it.

How do I add a skill?

Open the app, click "Add Skill", type the skill name (e.g., "Photography"), optionally add a short description, and save. It appears instantly.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All your skills are stored locally on your Windows PC. The app does not send anything over the internet unless you choose to share.

Can I uninstall it later?

Sure. Go to Windows Settings > Apps > bbshare-skills > Uninstall. Your exported files will remain.

🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

  • App won't open? Make sure your Windows is updated. Try running the app as Administrator (right-click > Run as administrator).
  • Download fails? Disable antivirus temporarily during download, or try a different browser (Edge or Chrome recommended).
  • Missing skills? Check if your data file is in the same folder as the app. If you moved it, the app may not find it.
